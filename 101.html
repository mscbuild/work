Mini games

Ready for a quick adventure? Our mini-games are perfect for a short break: easy controls, instant fun and zero downloads. Jump in, play for a couple of minutes and squeeze every drop of fun out of your free time — on desktop or mobile, right in your browser.

What are genres of mini-games?

Most mini-games are quick arcade games with simple controls. In many of them you race to the end of a level, dodge obstacles or react at lightning speed — no tutorial needed. Others are clever puzzles and skill games where a bit of logic and a steady hand take you to the top of the board.

Popular mini-games on our site

Fans of fast action love Geometry Jump — dodge the spikes and fly over every obstacle — and Helix Jump, where you bounce down a colorful tower. Want to laugh instead? Basket Random and Slap and Run serve chaotic ragdoll physics and guaranteed giggles. Puzzle lovers can relax with Marble Sort or Sort Tiles. Try any of them and fill your day with quick, funny games!
