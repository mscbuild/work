Our Story

A Family Tradition of
Baking Excellence

For over seven decades, Pâté Bakery has been the heart of our community. What began as a small family shop in 1952 has blossomed into a beloved destination for lovers of authentic French baking.

Every morning at 4 AM, our master bakers begin their craft, kneading dough by hand, shaping croissants with care, and baking breads that fill the streets with the irresistible aroma of fresh pastry. We use only organic flour, European butter, and seasonal fruits—because great baking starts with great ingredients.
